# Decisions and Constraints

| ID | Decision or constraint | Rationale | Source turn | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-001 | The application scope includes both the owner's own paving projects and asphalt supplied to external individuals and companies. | These are the plant's operating modes and both require records. | Turn 4 | Confirmed |
| DEC-002 | Outside-customer truck collection includes empty-truck weighing before loading and loaded-truck weighing after loading. | The two measurements support recording the quantity supplied. | Turn 6 | Confirmed |
| DEC-003 | A completed outside-customer load requires two documents: a delivery authorization and a receipt. | The delivery authorization captures transport and weight details, while the receipt captures net weight and price. | Turn 7 | Confirmed |
| DEC-004 | The price per ton is variable and the application must allow it to be changed. | Per-ton prices may differ between transactions or circumstances. | Turn 8 | Confirmed |
| DEC-005 | Only the plant owner acting as manager may change the price per ton, and a changed value becomes the default for future sales until changed again. | Pricing control is a managerial responsibility and the value persists across transactions. | Turn 9 | Confirmed |
| DEC-006 | Every asphalt load or project activity must be recorded, whether it is for the owner's own project or an outside project/customer. | The owner corrected the earlier interpretation that detailed recording applied only to outside sales. | Turn 10 | Confirmed |
| DEC-007 | Loads for the owner's projects follow the same customer workflow as outside sales, with the owner's company recorded as the customer. | The owner does not expect a process difference between own and outside work. | Turn 11 | Confirmed |
| DEC-008 | The receipt price may be left empty for loads serving the owner's projects. | Own-project loads do not require an outside-customer payment amount. | Turn 12 | Confirmed |
| DEC-009 | Weighbridge readings are manually entered in kilograms; net weight is calculated as full weight minus empty weight and converted automatically to tons when a conversion is set. | The system must derive net and ton values from manually entered readings. | Turn 13 | Confirmed |
| DEC-010 | Asphalt uses 1,000 kg = 1 ton, and the system shall allow selection of a configurable conversion rate and output unit for other material contexts. | Possible materials such as concrete use a different reporting unit. | Turn 14 | Confirmed; concrete scope pending |
| DEC-011 | Both asphalt and concrete are in scope for the first version and use the same basic weighing, recording, and receipt/invoice workflow. | The owner wants a simple common process for both materials. | Turn 15 | Confirmed |
| DEC-012 | Staff manually select asphalt or concrete for each load. The earlier interpretation that this alone automatically selects one conversion was superseded in Turn 19. | Material must remain explicit on every load. | Turns 18–19 | Partially superseded |
| DEC-013 | Users preparing a receipt manually select an appropriate conversion rate from saved conversion-rate options. | More than one conversion may be needed, especially for concrete. | Turn 19 | Confirmed |
| DEC-014 | The manager and authorized staff may add or change saved conversion-rate options. For version one, only the owner exists as a user; staff support is deferred. | The permission model was simplified by the single-user release boundary. | Turns 20 and 46 | Superseded for version one |
| DEC-015 | The system shall support user-defined categories and items and shall not be limited to asphalt and concrete. | The business may need to track additional products, materials, or activities. | Turn 21 | Confirmed |
| DEC-016 | Incoming quarry purchases shall be tracked by truck, quantity, payment status, and associated details and history. | The owner needs visibility into purchased quarry material and whether it has been paid. | Turn 21 | Confirmed at scope level |
| DEC-017 | The system shall not execute or process payments; all payments occur in person and the application only records and tracks them. | The product is a management and tracking tool, not a payment platform. | Turn 22 | Confirmed |
| DEC-018 | Each customer profile shall summarize total quantity taken and total price and provide an order history showing paid/unpaid status, paid amount, payment date, and remaining balance. | The owner needs customer-level commercial visibility and traceable order details. | Turn 23 | Confirmed |
| DEC-019 | Customer orders may be partially paid and retain a remaining balance. | Customers are not required to settle the full order at once. | Turn 24 | Confirmed |
| DEC-020 | Each partial payment is retained as a separate amount-and-date entry linked to the specific customer order it pays. | A customer can have several orders and each order needs an independent payment history and balance. | Turn 25 | Confirmed |
| DEC-021 | Incoming quarry deliveries record quantity directly and do not require empty and full weighbridge entries in the application. | The owner uses a simpler purchase-record workflow for quarry trucks. | Turn 26 | Confirmed |
| DEC-022 | Quarry delivery quantity is recorded in cubic metres, and the system shall support configurable measurement units that can be added as settings. | The business needs flexible units for user-defined items. | Turn 27 | Confirmed |
| DEC-023 | Categories, items, measurement units, and conversion rates are managed by the owner in version one; staff management may be added later. | Version one is single-user. | Turns 28 and 46 | Confirmed as revised |
| DEC-024 | Each quarry-truck delivery records the driver and vehicle plate number in addition to its quarry, item, quantity, and payment information. | The owner needs truck-level delivery traceability. | Turn 29 | Confirmed |
| DEC-025 | Quarry delivery records shall not update inventory or stock; they are retained only for quantity and payment tracking. | The owner does not need inventory management for quarry deliveries. | Turn 30 | Confirmed |
| DEC-026 | Fuel stock shall be tracked, including the machine filled and fuel amount for each refuelling. | The owner needs visibility into current fuel and machine consumption. | Turn 32 | Confirmed at scope level |
| DEC-027 | A project foreman shall be able to create work reports covering what happened, who attended, and materials used or transported with quantities. | The owner/foreman needs a historical operational account of project work. | Turn 32 | Confirmed at scope level |
| DEC-028 | One foreman report shall be created for each project workday and cover the activity of that day. | Daily project history is the required reporting period. | Turn 33 | Confirmed |
| DEC-029 | The foreman shall manually enter workers, drivers, trucks, and machines present in the daily report rather than being required to select saved profiles. | The owner wants simple, flexible daily reporting. | Turn 34 | Confirmed |
| DEC-030 | Daily report presence entries identify workers and drivers by name, trucks by plate number, and machines by name. | These are the minimum identifiers needed to recognize entries later. | Turn 36 | Confirmed |
| DEC-031 | Each daily-report material entry records the selected item, quantity, unit, and whether the material was used or transported. | The foreman needs structured quantities for project history and reporting. | Turn 37 | Confirmed |
| DEC-032 | Daily project reports remain editable and do not require a separate manager approval or locking step; the owner currently acts as both manager and foreman. | A separate approval workflow is unnecessary for the current operating model. | Turn 38 | Confirmed |
| DEC-033 | A fuel delivery is recorded by entering the number of litres delivered, which increases recorded fuel stock. | The owner wants a minimal stock-in workflow. | Turn 39 | Confirmed |
| DEC-034 | Fuel consumption shall be recorded only for machines; plant fuel consumption is not tracked. | The owner cannot measure the plant's consumption. | Turn 40 | Confirmed constraint |
| DEC-035 | After physically checking the fuel tank, the owner may manually correct the recorded fuel balance to the actual litre amount. | Manual reconciliation compensates for untracked plant consumption. | Turns 41 and 46 | Confirmed for version one |
| DEC-036 | The consolidated scope through configurable records, orders/payments, quarry tracking, fuel tracking/correction, and daily project reports is accepted as accurate. | The owner confirmed the fourth elicitation checkpoint. | Turn 42 | Confirmed |
| DEC-037 | The home screen shall prominently provide a “Make Receipt” action and offer all known dashboard summaries with configurable visibility. | Receipt creation is the highest-priority action, while users need control over displayed information. | Turn 44 | Confirmed |
| DEC-038 | Version one shall have one application user, the owner; staff accounts are deferred to a later release. | The owner wants the initial product for personal use while preserving future expansion. | Turn 46 | Confirmed |
| DEC-039 | Version one shall be phone-first and support printing receipts to a Bluetooth POS printer. | The owner needs mobile operation at the plant/projects and immediate physical receipt output. | Turn 47 | Confirmed |
| DEC-040 | Version one shall support both Android phones and iPhones. | The owner needs cross-platform phone access. | Turn 48 | Confirmed |
| DEC-041 | Receipt printing shall support configurable paper widths and shall not be fixed to the current small Xprinter format. | The owner wants compatibility with different POS printer sizes. | Turn 49 | Confirmed at capability level |
| DEC-042 | The existing Xprinter uses a 58 mm paper roll; its exact model and printable width remain unverified. | The owner clarified what the photographed 58 mm label represents. | Turn 51 | Confirmed at paper-size level |
| DEC-043 | Version one shall print to both the existing Xprinter Android POS terminal and compatible separate portable Bluetooth printers. | The owner needs to use existing equipment and portable printing options. | Turn 52 | Confirmed |
| DEC-044 | A receipt shall be saved even when no printer is connected or printing fails, and it may be printed repeatedly while open. | Record creation must not depend on printer availability. | Turn 53 | Confirmed; later reprint scope pending |
| DEC-045 | Saved receipts may be reopened from history and reprinted later without a fixed copy limit. | The owner needs durable receipt records and flexible replacement copies. | Turn 54 | Confirmed |
| DEC-046 | The single-user mobile and printing scope summarized through Turn 55 is accepted as accurate. | The owner confirmed the fifth elicitation checkpoint. | Turn 55 | Confirmed |

| DEC-048 | Offline changes shall synchronize automatically when internet connectivity returns and become available on another owner device. | The owner needs continuity across phones without manual data transfer. | Turn 57 | Confirmed |
| DEC-049 | Installing the app and signing in on a replacement phone shall automatically restore all synchronized data. | The owner needs recovery from phone loss or replacement. | Turn 58 | Confirmed |
| DEC-050 | The owner shall sign in using an email address and password. | The owner selected a cross-platform authentication method. | Turn 59 | Confirmed |
| DEC-051 | A forgotten password shall be reset using a secure link sent to the owner's email address. | The owner needs self-service account recovery. | Turn 60 | Confirmed |
| DEC-052 | After initial sign-in, the app shall remain accessible offline without requesting the account password each time it opens. | Repeated password prompts would obstruct offline field use. | Turn 61 | Confirmed |
| DEC-053 | Version one shall not include an app-specific fingerprint, Face ID, or PIN lock. | The owner does not require an additional local lock. | Turn 62 | Confirmed |
| DEC-054 | Version one shall not include remote lost-device sign-out. | Superseded by the explicit correction in Turn 64. | Turn 63 | Superseded |

| DEC-057 | Reports shall support applicable filters for date range, category/item, customer/project, and paid/unpaid status before export. | The owner needs targeted operational and financial analysis. | Turn 66 | Confirmed |
| DEC-058 | If synchronization fails, local data shall remain safe, be visibly marked pending, and retry automatically later. | Connectivity problems must not cause data loss or require manual recovery. | Turn 67 | Confirmed |
| DEC-059 | If the same record is edited offline on two owner devices, the newest edit shall automatically replace the older version. | The owner chose a simple single-user conflict rule. | Turn 69 | Confirmed |
| DEC-060 | An issued receipt shall be corrected by directly editing it; cancel-and-replace is not required. | The owner explicitly corrected the initial “both” response to prefer direct correction. | Turn 70 | Confirmed |
