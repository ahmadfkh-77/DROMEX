1. Arrived today

A company-wide card after the places: what came INTO the company's stock that day, apart from supplier loads (which stay in each project). Three kinds: Stock receipts (batches received in Stock), Diesel deliveries (tank deliveries, litres, supplier, ticket) and Bitumen deliveries (kg, supplier, grade), each its own table. Bitumen used is not repeated here: it stays in the asphalt tables. Cancelled deliveries are excluded; nothing unrecorded is shown; no zero rows; units never added together. Fuel arrived also appears in "Totals by material", next to fuel given.

2. Issues and Daily Reports

A company-wide card for the day's issues and daily reports, as a list only (never opened or changed from here). Issues created that day (title, project, priority, status Open or Resolved) and Daily Reports for that day (project, who prepared it, how many photos and notes). Photos and long notes are counted, not printed. Tapping a row opens the issue or the report. A project without a daily report that day is not listed as missing here (Needs Attention already does that).
