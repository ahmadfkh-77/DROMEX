What happens: DROMEX closes for a few seconds while Android swaps the icon, then you open it again. Some phones take a moment to refresh the home screen. Your data is not touched.

Limit: Android only allows ready-made icons that are built into the app. You cannot use your own picture as the app icon. To add a new icon later, I add it and make a new build.

What this changes and what it doesn't

  • Needs Attention keeps every count, every group and every link. The same items open the same screens. Only the look and the grouping change. Draft Center moves into the Home card.
  • Skyline choices are saved on the phone as a setting, so no database change. They are included in your backups and survive restarts and offline use. Hidden removes the picture and the header closes up.
  • App icon is the one part that needs a new APK build (I won't make one without your approval) because the icons are packed into the app. In-place install keeps your data, as before.
