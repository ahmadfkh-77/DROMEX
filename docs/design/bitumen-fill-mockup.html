Bitumen: Record Fill (mockup v1)

The idea: a truck is filled with bitumen straight from a batch to be used on the road (not through an asphalt load). You record the fill and the batch page shows how much has been taken, by day, next to the amounts the asphalt loads use. Drawn with the existing diesel Record Fill and batch page design (0.20.0), the bitumen batch screen, and the colours from the bitumen use-by-day mockup. Figures are invented.

1 · Batch page, with fillsNew: "Record Fill" button, "Taken by fills" figure, and a "Fills by day" list. The existing "Use from asphalt loads" section stays as it is.

What I assumed (tell me what to change)

  1. Remaining = delivered + adjustments − fills − estimated use from asphalt loads. Both come out of the same batch.
  2. A fill is in kg, picks the oldest open batch first, and splits across two batches when the first runs out (same as diesel).
  3. You choose a truck (saved trucks, as for diesel) and a destination (project, company site or unassigned).
  4. A fill is never edited or deleted silently. Wrong fills are cancelled with a reason and stay on record, like diesel.
  5. Fills appear in the batch PDF as their own section, next to the asphalt use by day.
