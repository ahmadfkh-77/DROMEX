Bitumen Batch Report: Use from asphalt loads by day (PDF mockup)

Only the "Use from asphalt loads" section changes; the rest of the report is as it is today. Loads are grouped by the day they were confirmed (local time), newest day first, matching the batch page (DEC-542). Every day shows its total asphalt mix (t) and its total bitumen (kg). The closing row stays and equals the sum of the days (DEC-527). Same example figures as the screen mockup: rule 40 kg per 1 t.

Option A (recommended) · a tinted day row with the day's totals above its loadsThe day's totals sit in the same Mix and Bitumen columns as the loads, so the eye runs down the columns. If a day breaks over a page, the table header repeats and the totals are already at the top of the day. The Date column is replaced by Time because the date is in the day row.
