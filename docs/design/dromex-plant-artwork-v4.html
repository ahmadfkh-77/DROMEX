DROMEX — LOGO CONCEPTS v4

Realistic plants: a working asphalt batch plant, a concrete batching plant and a quarry, drawn flat in navy and orange

THE THREE PLANTS

Quarry

Benched pit with rock layers, an excavator digging on the bench, a haul truck on the pit floor, the primary crusher on its steel frame, the conveyor and the stone stockpile.

Asphalt batch plant

Four cold feed bins, the charging belt, the orange drier drum with riding rings and burner, the hot elevator, the mixing tower (screen house, hot bins, weigh hopper, pugmill) with a truck loading under it, the baghouse, stack and bitumen tank.

Concrete batching plant

Three cement silos on braced legs with dust filters, screw conveyors into the mixer tower, a water tank on the roof, a mixer truck loading, the covered aggregate belt and the aggregate bins with their weigh belt.

THE FULL SKYLINE

Two shades of navy and two of orange give the silos, drum and tanks a rounded, real look while staying flat (no gradients), so it still prints, paints and embroiders cleanly. For very small uses (app icon, 24 px) the D monogram or a single plant reads better than the full skyline.

LOGO LAYOUTS

1 · Primary stacked

Documents, PDFs and receipt headers.

2 · Badge (evolves the current logo)

Keeps the navy rounded frame and orange panel from today's logo.

3 · Shield crest

The shield carries the asphalt plant at full detail. Works on helmets and vests.

4 · Round seal

Stamp for receipts, delivery authorizations and certificates.

5 · D monogram

The asphalt plant sits inside the letter D. Becomes the app icon.

6 · Divisions

One brand, three business units. Each tile can head its own reports.

7 · Dark signage

Site boards, splash screen, night-time visibility.

8 · Truck door / site banner

Hazard stripe band for trucks, machines and site hoarding.

APP ICONS & STAMPS

Phone app icon (first three), single-plant icons and a round stamp.
