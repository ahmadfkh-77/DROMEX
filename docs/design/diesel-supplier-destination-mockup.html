Update batch 2 — Diesel supplier, batch editing, date range, and Supplier Load destination: proposed design

Sample data only. Everything uses the existing DROMEX components: the Fuel Management tabs, day cards, SearchableSelect, DatePickerField, the collapsed orange filter card, navy group headers, the shared export panel and the A4 report style. Screens marked NEW or CHANGED are what would change; anything not shown stays exactly as it is.

A · Diesel batch: supplier, invoice number and optional price

Request 1 and 2. A batch is created by Record Delivery (the Purchase tab). The supplier picker is the same one already used for supplier loads. Price stays optional: empty saves the batch as Unpriced; the app never shows a $0 price. Only a priced purchase needs a supplier, because the amount is owed to that supplier (existing rule).

E · Supplier Loads → Purchase history → open a supplier: NEW Export PDF

Today, opening a supplier shows its projects but offers no export (only Invoices & Bills and Edit supplier information). Add Export PDF there: it exports everything delivered by that supplier, grouped by project or site. The sheet lets you choose materials, destination, dates and the header (logo, name, details). The PDF reads Totals with the supplier's name in bold and lists every load with its number and prefix.
