Mockup v3 - Asphalt Calculator as separate pages (nothing is built yet). You chose Direction Y and said it still looked like one page. So now every step is its own page, like New Supplier Load:
  • Each page has its own coloured header band with a big step number, a title and one line of help: 1 · Area (navy), 2 · Rate (orange), 3 · Result (teal). The next page looks clearly different when you press Next.
  • Progress dots and Back / Next in the bottom bar, the same as Supplier Load. The bar also shows the figures so far (area, tonnes), so the answer is never hidden.
  • Step 3 is the answer: one large teal number (planned tonnes) with the rest in a ruled list, then Save. More tools and Saved calculations are separate pages opened from step 3, each with its own band, so the seven tools are no longer on the same scroll.
  • Each tool is its own page with a band, its fields, and its results.
  • Inside a page: Open form (underlined 18 px fields, small group labels, hairlines, no boxes, no stripes).
Road sections sit on page 1, with the area rule note always visible above the first section. Sample: 10 m (4 → 4.4 m) = 42.0 m², 20 m (4.4 → 6 m) = 104.0 m², total 146.0 m². This replaces the closed-by-default sections of DEC-345 and the filled teal cards of DEC-343 (teal stays for the derived numbers and the hero figure); I'll record that as a new decision only if you approve.

Direct area
Section area = Length × (Width 1 + Width 2) ÷ 2. Example: 10 × (4 + 4.4) ÷ 2 = 42.0 m². Total area = the sum of all section areas. Straight edges are assumed inside each section: if the road bends or the width changes unevenly, split it into more sections.
