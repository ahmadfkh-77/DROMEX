A. Prices switch (PDF with prices ON)

Prices stay OFF by default. When ON, the PDF header says "Prices included" instead of "Quantities only, no prices", and Price and Amount columns appear only in tables where at least one price was recorded. Money totals per currency (never mixed). A record without a recorded price shows "Unpriced" (never 0) and is counted in the total row.

B. Contract target (per project)

Each project card on the preview has its own switch right under the name, OFF by default and remembered per project. ON adds a "Contract target" table for that project from Contract Quantities: contract quantity, used before today, used today, % used, left. Used = company loads plus supplier loads delivered to the project plus reasoned adjustments (the same figures as the Quantity Targets page). OFF: no table, and that project's block says "Contract target not included". A project with no contract quantities shows the switch disabled: "Needs contract quantities for this project".

C. Attendance sheet (who worked where, no hours, no pay)

A simple daily presence sheet, needs your confirmation that it supersedes DEC-201 only for presence and places (no hours, no wages, no pay). Three lists from your existing records: People (drivers and operators), Trucks, Machines. Per day each has a "worked today" tick and one or more places: a project, a company site, or a typed place for that day only.

D. Excel workbook (already built)

Sheets: Summary (date, key figures, company loads, supplier loads and fuel by material and unit), one sheet per place with records (Record ID, Record Type, Date, Time, Load No., Material, Unit, Quantity as a number, Details, Truck, Driver, Delivered By, Ticket, Bitumen Used kg), and a Data Dictionary. Later phases add the sheets "Attendance" (when its switch is ON), "Contract target" (projects with the switch ON) and Price columns (only when Prices is ON).
