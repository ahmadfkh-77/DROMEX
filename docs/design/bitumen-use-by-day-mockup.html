Use from asphalt loads, by day (mockup v2)

Rendered at 390 px. Tap a day header to try it. Figures are examples with the rule 40 kg per 1 t.

What changed from v1: (1) the strip is one line of three figures with dividers, so it reads left to right: loads, mix, bitumen. (2) Colour key used everywhere: navy = asphalt mix (t), orange = bitumen (kg), so the two never get confused. (3) The old "Estimated use / Asphalt mix made" lines at the bottom are removed because the strip says the same; the strip carries "Estimate" wording. (4) Load rows are tighter: name and time on one line, the two figures under it, the rule and Move on one line. (5) Day header gives the date and load count only; the totals sit directly under it in the same two columns the loads use, so columns line up from day to day.
