Own company as the Project Company: proposed design

Sample data only. Your own company is saved as a customer marked "own company". It is now offered in the Project Company setup, selected by default, and its projects' PDFs start with the Project Company header. The Plant Company (the supplier, from Company Settings) is unchanged and stays selectable. Existing saved setups, customers and records are never changed.
