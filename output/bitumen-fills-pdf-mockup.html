Bitumen Batch Report: Fills (PDF mockup v1)

A batch that has fills gets one new section, FILLS INTO TRUCKS, placed directly before USE FROM ASPHALT LOADS, plus a new Taken by fills figure. Everything else keeps today's look (DEC-520 family, same bands, same strip, same footer). Fills are grouped by the day chosen on the fill (newest first), like the asphalt use, with the day total in a tinted row. Cancelled fills are listed, struck through and never counted. A batch with no fills prints exactly as it does today. Figures are invented and match the screen mockup (30,000 delivered, 8,400 by fills, 14,950 by asphalt, −150 adjustment, 6,500 left).
